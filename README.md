THE IMPACT OF SOCIAL MEDIA USAGE ON ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE: A
CASE STUDY OF KNUST STUDENTS
ABSTRACT
This paper aims to assess the impact of social media usage on academic performance of
students of Kwame Nkrumah
4
University of Science and Technology, Ghana. In this paper, the researchers are going to find out
the real impact of social media usage on KNUST students’ academic performance. Descriptive
online survey questionnaire was conducted to show the real image of
circumstances. Information on 333 students from all colleges of the university were randomly
selected as the respondents of the study. It was conducted during the second semester in
2024. This study found out that social media is a part of students’ life. Nonetheless, it is of little
impact on students’ academic performance. It helps to
improve students’ academic performance when it is used for academic purposes but tend to
decrease students’ grades when it is used for entertainment and leisure. In addition,
through social media platforms students communicate with
each other, seeking for career opportunities and social support
and campus life and events. The study found that on average students spend 4.77 hours on social
media with a standard deviation of 2.85 hours, indicating that there is some variability in
how much time students spend on social media. Some students spend much less or much
more time than the average.
Finally, it can be conducted that social media has a slight negative impact on students’ academic
performance. Usage of social media is important for students if they channel the time
they spend on social media mainly on academic purposes.
